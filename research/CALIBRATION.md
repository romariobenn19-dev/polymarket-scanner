# Are Polymarket prices wrong anywhere?

5,671 resolved yes/no markets from 2,375 events (last 12 months, $5k+ volume, chosen by end date, most recent first, so outcomes can't affect which markets are included).
For each, the YES price 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days before its scheduled end, compared with what happened.
A market only counts at a point in time if it was still open then, using its hourly price from no more than 2 hours earlier (no stale prices, no hindsight).

**How to read it:** *Price* is what the market said; *Happened* is how often YES actually won. If *Happened* is lower than *Price*, YES was overpriced and buying NO had an edge. Numbers are averaged per event (a 20-candidate election counts once), with ± = 2 standard errors. A gap is only meaningful when it is bigger than its ±.
